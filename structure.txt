🧠 Why This Is Important

Without autoscaling:
High Traffic
     │
     ▼
1 Pod
     │
❌ Slow Response
❌ Overloaded CPU

With HPA:
High Traffic
      │
      ▼
Horizontal Pod Autoscaler
      │
      ▼
1 Pod → 3 Pods → 5 Pods

As traffic decreases:

Low Traffic
      │
      ▼
5 Pods → 3 Pods → 1 Pod

Benefits:
✅ Automatic scaling
✅ Better performance
✅ Cost optimization
✅ High availability
✅ Production-ready deployments
